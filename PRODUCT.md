# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who already track films on Letterboxd or similar services and want one calm, local place to import that history, understand it visually, and find their next film.

## Product Purpose

Osvell turns an existing film history into a private, polished library with useful visual context and grounded discovery. Success is a user being able to quickly recognize their taste and confidently choose something new to watch.

## Brand Personality

Modern, clean, and cool. The experience should feel simple and deliberately composed: Apple TV-level restraint and material quality without imitating a streaming service.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI-dashboard aesthetics, busy data walls, and another social logging site. Glass should clarify utility and hierarchy, never become decoration or obscure film art.

## Design Principles

- Treat imported history as the source of truth, not a reason to recreate logging workflows.
- Let film artwork and clear editorial hierarchy do the expressive work.
- Keep controls familiar, reachable, and visually quiet.
- Make discovery feel concise and explainable, with reasons beside each recommendation.
- Prefer a small, consistent material vocabulary over novelty.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA contrast expectations, preserve visible keyboard focus, respect reduced-motion and reduced-transparency preferences, and never rely on color alone to communicate state.
